# TounsiWave Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

TounsiWave is an ad-free radio player for listening to Tunisian and other curated radio stations. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Data We Collect

TounsiWave does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling SDKs.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Preferences such as favorite stations are stored locally on your device. TounsiWave does not send these preferences to the developer or synchronize them with an external account.

## Network Connections and Third-Party Services

TounsiWave connects to the Radio Browser directory to discover station information and connects directly to radio stations and related content providers to load audio streams and station images. These third-party servers receive the technical information normally required to deliver content over the internet, such as an IP address and basic request information. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices.

TounsiWave does not use information from these connections for advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling.

## Children's Privacy

TounsiWave does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be available at this URL, with its effective date shown above.
